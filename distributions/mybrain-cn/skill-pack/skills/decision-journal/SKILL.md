# Decision journal

## Job

Preserve the choice, the evidence that earned it, and what would reverse it.

## Capture

1. Decision and accountable owner.
2. Alternatives considered, including the strongest rejected option.
3. Evidence and assumptions.
4. Expected consequence and named failure cost.
5. Reversal condition or review trigger.
6. Source/provenance and decision date.

Use `remember` for atomic facts and commitments. File a decision page when the reasoning needs to survive beyond one fact. A later correction must supersede the old judgment rather than silently overwrite history.
