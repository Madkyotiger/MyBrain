# Commitment tracker

## Job

Make commitments retrievable as obligations, not buried lines in meeting notes.

## Capture

For each commitment record: what, owner, beneficiary, due/review date, status, provenance, and dependency. Use `remember` with `kind: commitment` and an entity whenever one is known.

## Review

1. Recall active commitments for the person/project.
2. Separate overdue, at risk, waiting, and completed.
3. Flag ownerless or date-less commitments as incomplete records.
4. Do not infer completion from silence.
5. End with the one follow-up that changes closure.
