# Project brief

## Job

Reconstruct the project as a decision surface: objective, evidence, assumptions, decisions, reversal conditions, and next move.

## Steps

1. Recall pages and facts for the project and involved entities.
2. State the real objective and current success condition.
3. Split evidence from assumptions and interpretations.
4. List decisions already made, including owner and date when known.
5. Name unresolved choices, reversal conditions, and the next owner-ready action.

## Output

Use: objective → current truth → tension → decisions → unknowns → next move. Do not repeat old Why when the room now needs How.
