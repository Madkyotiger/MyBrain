# Meeting prep

## Job

Make the user enter one meeting with restored context and one deliberate outcome.

## Steps

1. Recall the person, organization, project, and recent meeting names.
2. Pull recent changes and active commitments; do not summarize the whole history.
3. Distinguish what the other side wants, what the user wants, and where they may diverge.
4. Name one value the user can bring and one question that could change the meeting.
5. Expose missing or stale context instead of filling it in.

## Output

- **This meeting needs to move:** one result.
- **Recent context:** 3–5 sourced points.
- **Open commitments:** owner and status.
- **Useful tension:** disagreement, risk, or hidden choice.
- **Unknown:** what must be confirmed in the room.
- **First move:** the opening question or action.
